If you forget your password, Invoksa uses a two-step email confirmation flow to verify your identity before letting you set a new one. First, you request a reset email; then, once you've confirmed your identity, you submit a new password. The process takes less than a minute.

## Reset Password Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a password reset email">
    Send a `POST` request to `/api/auth/confirm-pwd/{email}`, replacing `{email}` with the address registered to your account.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/auth/confirm-pwd/you@example.com
    ```

    **Response:**

    ```json
    "Mail de confirmation envoyé"
    ```

    This confirms that a reset email has been dispatched. If the email address is not found in the system, no email will be sent — double-check the address you used to register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Open your inbox and look for a reset confirmation email from Invoksa. Follow any instructions or links contained in the email. If you don't see it within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your new password">
    Once you've confirmed your identity via email, send a `POST` request to `/api/auth/reset-pwd` with your email address and your chosen new password.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/auth/reset-pwd \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "email": "you@example.com",
        "newPassword": "myNewSecurePassword1"
      }'
    ```

    A successful response confirms the password has been updated. You can now log in with your new credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Password Requirements

<Note>
  Your new password must be **at least 6 characters** long. There are no additional complexity requirements, but we recommend using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for a stronger password.
</Note>

---

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="I didn't receive the reset email">
  Check your **spam or junk folder** first — automated emails sometimes land there. Also verify that you're checking the inbox for the exact email address you registered with in Invoksa. If the address doesn't match any account, the system silently skips sending the email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My new password is rejected">
  Make sure your new password is **at least 6 characters long**. Passwords shorter than 6 characters are rejected by the API. Try a longer password and resubmit the request.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I still can't log in after resetting">
  If you reset your password successfully but still can't log in, try the following:

  - Confirm you're using the correct email address.
  - Check that you're not accidentally including extra spaces in your password.
  - Request another reset email via `POST /api/auth/confirm-pwd/{email}` and go through the flow again.
  - If the problem persists, contact your Invoksa administrator.
</Accordion>
