Whether you're building a billing dashboard, automating invoice delivery, or integrating invoicing into an existing product, Invoksa gives you the endpoints you need to get it done.

## How Invoksa Works

Get up and running in three steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register & Verify Your Email">
    Create your account with a username, email, and password. Invoksa sends a verification email — click the link before you log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Exchange your credentials for a JWT Bearer token. Include that token in the `Authorization` header of every subsequent request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Making API Calls">
    Create clients, build invoices with line items, export PDFs, share public invoice links, and generate invoices from text using AI.
  </Step>
</Steps>
